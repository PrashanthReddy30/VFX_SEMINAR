02 SIDE BY SIDE

Resume vs portfolio

Both matter. One introduces you; the other puts your craft on screen.

Resume

The introduction
A resume tells.
  • Lists your experience and training.
  • Names the tools and skills you use.
  • Summarizes your roles and background.

Portfolio

The proof
A portfolio shows.
  • Shows the shots you actually created.
  • Reveals your eye for detail and finish.
  • Explains your contribution with breakdowns.
